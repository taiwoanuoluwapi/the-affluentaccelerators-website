The Market Reality

Businesses Need People Who Can Think, Analyse, And Solve Problems.

Businesses do not struggle simply from a lack of effort; they struggle from operational friction, flawed pricing models, inefficient processes, and an inability to diagnose why growth has stalled. Organisations desperately need professionals who can understand how enterprises operate, identify inefficiencies, interpret financial and commercial data, and formulate practical, executable solutions.

The 90-Day Programme is designed as an intensive, practical pathway to equip you with exactly these high-value capabilities.

Comprehensive Syllabus

What You Will Learn

A 12-module structured curriculum bridging consulting theory with hands-on analytical execution. Click each module to view details.

Understanding the consulting industry architecture, role of strategic advisory, trusted advisor mindset, ethics, client expectations, and problem-solving methodologies used by premier consulting firms.
Deconstructing business models (Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Canvas), revenue engines, margin structures, cost drivers, and organizational governance frameworks.
Primary & secondary commercial research techniques, industry benchmarking, competitor intelligence, TAM/SAM market sizing, and customer interview methodologies.
Isolating symptoms from root causes, issue tree structures, hypothesis-driven problem solving, MECE frameworks, and organizational diagnostic tools.
Interpreting Income Statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow dynamics. Analyzing unit economics (CAC, LTV, contribution margins), pricing leverage, and capital expenditure impact.
Process mapping, identifying operational bottlenecks, cycle time reduction, standard operating procedure (SOP) design, and workflow automation opportunities.
Conversion funnels, sales pipelines, channel partner economics, retention loops, and growth marketing infrastructure required to sustain commercial traction.
Crafting competitive advantage, blue ocean opportunities, partnership structuring, forward vertical integration, and commercial roadmap prioritization.
Pyramid principle of executive communication, writing rigorous consulting deliverables, data visualization, executive summaries, and board-ready slide decks.
Stakeholder management, conducting discovery workshops, handling client objections, scope management, and high-stakes negotiation skills.
Conducting live, in-field assessments on assigned local and regional enterprises. Synthesizing real operational observations into actionable diagnostic audit sheets.
Formulating practical implementation roadmaps, change management protocols, governance milestones, and presenting final strategic findings to business leadership.

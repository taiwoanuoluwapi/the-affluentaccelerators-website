Got Questions?

Candidate Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our recruitment, commitment, and career growth model.

Who is eligible to apply for the Intern and Fellowship roles?

We welcome motivated students in their final years, active NYSC corp members, fresh university graduates, and early-career professionals looking to pivot into strategic business consulting, executive operations, and commercial development. What matters most is intellectual curiosity, proactive initiative, and coachability.

Is the internship remote, in-person, or hybrid?

We operate a flexible hybrid model. Key strategic review sessions, partner briefings, and client presentations take place through our hub and strategic sessions in Lagos, combined with remote operational sprints that allow flexible, high-focus contribution.

What is the duration of the internship track?

Our practical internship and fellowship tracks range from 3 to 6 months depending on the specific role and agreement. High-performing interns who exhibit consistent ownership and analytical rigor are offered accelerated transitions into full-time Associate Consultant positions.

How do I submit my application for the active openings?

You can apply directly through our official Google Forms for either the Business Development Intern or Executive Assistant Intern role linked on this page. Alternatively, you can use our Internship Application Form on the website.

What makes a candidate stand out during selection?

Candidates who stand out articulate their motivation clearly, show evidence of self-driven curiosity about business models, write persuasively, and demonstrate an eagerness to contribute actively rather than simply waiting for instructions.

Business Development Intern

"Curious about business and love to help develop businesses? Join our team at The Affluentaccelerators to learn, contribute, and build real businesses."

About The Role

As a Business Development Intern at The Affluentaccelerators, you will sit at the frontlines of our market expansion and commercial growth. You will collaborate directly with our consulting leadership to identify new client engagements, map business sectors, build partnership outreach, and develop practical commercial solutions.

Your Core Responsibilities

  • Identify business opportunities: Proactively research emerging market dynamics, growing businesses, and untapped commercial advisory opportunities.
  • Research & initiate outreach: Map key decision-makers, craft structured proposals, and initiate introductory communications.
  • Drive results and create value: Support client discovery calls, draft presentation decks, and contribute to commercial campaigns.
  • Maintain growth systems: Track prospect interactions, lead conversion metrics, and partner databases.

Who We Are Looking For

  • Curious, ambitious self-starters passionate about business strategy, sales, and entrepreneurship.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills with a confident, polished demeanor.
  • Comfortable with outreach, relationship building, and strategic research.
  • Students, NYSC corp members, recent graduates, or early-career professionals eager for practical corporate acceleration.

What You Will Gain

Hands-on exposure to active corporate consulting engagements, direct mentorship from seasoned business advisors, practical portfolio assets, and an inside track to advanced associate opportunities within our firm.

Executive Office • Open Role

Executive Assistant Intern

"Be the right hand in building businesses and impact. Support the Founder and leadership team at The Affluentaccelerators to stay organized, focused, and execute efficiently."

About The Role

As an Executive Assistant Intern, you will work closely with the Founder and senior executives at The Affluentaccelerators. You will be the operational backbone that keeps leadership schedules on point, tracks deliverables across client initiatives, and ensures flawless day-to-day administrative and strategic execution.

Your Core Responsibilities

  • Keep schedules & priorities on track: Manage calendar cadence, prioritize urgent inbound requests, and prepare executive briefings.
  • Coordinate tasks, projects & follow-ups: Act as liaison between the Founder and internal/external stakeholders to keep sprint deadlines moving.
  • Organize information & key documents: Systematically file client decks, meeting minutes, contracts, and advisory documents.
  • Support communication & stakeholder engagement: Draft crisp professional emails, prepare presentation summaries, and manage correspondence.
  • Help drive execution across initiatives: Follow up on open action items, organize workshops, and ensure strategic commitments are met.

A Good Fit If You...

  • Are organized, highly proactive, and exceptionally reliable.
  • Communicate well, write clearly, and take initiative before being asked.
  • Enjoy supporting business, operational coordination, and project execution.
  • Are curious about business systems, high-level productivity, and executive leadership.

What You Will Gain

An unprecedented front-row seat to high-level strategic decision-making, executive operations, firm building, and direct personal mentorship from the Founder.

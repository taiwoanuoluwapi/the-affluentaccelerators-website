Strategic Curriculum

The Blueprint Behind Investment-Ready Businesses

Most businesses seek capital before building the systems that make capital multiply. This masterclass strips away the noise and teaches you the concrete operating machinery investors require before releasing capital.

01

De-risking Founder Dependency

Why businesses that rely purely on founder hustle get rejected by institutional funds, and how to institutionalize governance, decision rights, and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

02

Unit Economics & Margin Visibility

Transitioning from informal cash flow tracking to auditable unit contribution economics, cash cycle duration, and lifetime value vs. customer acquisition cost (LTV/CAC).

03

Corporate Governance & Compliance

Structuring corporate equity, shareholder agreements, clean capitalization tables, and legal compliance before opening conversations with angels, funds, or grant providers.

04

Live Diagnostics & Case Critique

Fulfilment Taiwo will conduct live diagnostic breakdowns on selected attendees' businesses, pinpointing commercial bottlenecks and offering real-time strategic prescriptions.

Yes, attendance for "The Investable Business Playbook" (Event 001) is 100% free of charge. However, attendance capacity on Google Meet is capped to preserve interactive Q&A quality.

How do I receive my Google Meet link?

Upon submitting the registration form, your unique Registration ID will be confirmed immediately. The official Google Meet room link and calendar invitation are delivered directly to your provided email and WhatsApp 24 hours and 1 hour prior to the session.
